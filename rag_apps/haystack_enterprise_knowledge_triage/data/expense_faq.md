# Expenses FAQ

## How do I claim travel expenses?
Submit receipts in the expense tool within 30 days of the trip. Expenses without receipts over 25 USD are rejected.

## What is the daily meal allowance when travelling?
The meal allowance is 60 USD per day for domestic travel and 85 USD per day for international travel.

## Do I need approval for flights?
Flights must be booked through the corporate travel portal. Economy class is the default; business class requires VP approval for flights longer than 6 hours.
