# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and MFA
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all corporate systems, including email, VPN and the code repository.

## Device security
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 1 hour.

## Data classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data may only be shared through approved tools and must never be sent to personal email accounts.
