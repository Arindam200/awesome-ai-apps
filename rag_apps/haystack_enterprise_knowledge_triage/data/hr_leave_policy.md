# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave must be requested at least 5 business days in advance through the HR portal and approved by the line manager. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks. Leave may be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
