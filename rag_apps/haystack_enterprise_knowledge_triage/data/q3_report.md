# Q3 Operations Report

## Highlights
Platform availability was 99.95% in Q3, above the 99.9% target. The number of SEV1 incidents fell from 4 in Q2 to 1 in Q3.

## Support
Median first-response time for support tickets improved from 4.2 hours to 2.8 hours. Customer satisfaction (CSAT) was 91%.

## Risks
The data-warehouse migration slipped by 3 weeks because of vendor delays and is now scheduled for completion in Q4.
