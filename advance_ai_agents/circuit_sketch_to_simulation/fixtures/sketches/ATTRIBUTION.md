## CGHD

From the CGHD dataset of hand-drawn circuit diagrams: J. Thoma, F. Bayer et al., "A Public Ground-Truth Dataset for Handwritten Circuit Diagram Images", ICDAR 2021.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6385813
- Mirror: https://huggingface.co/datasets/lowercaseonly/cghd

The Zenodo record lists CC BY 4.0, and the Hugging Face mirror states CC BY-SA 3.0. These files are used under the stricter of the two, CC BY-SA 3.0.
